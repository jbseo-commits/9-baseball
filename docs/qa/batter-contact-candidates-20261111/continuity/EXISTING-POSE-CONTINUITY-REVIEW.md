## Findings

The target-specific PRE cells are not three interchangeable copies: their helmet/head, trunk and pelvis descend in order high→middle→low while the two hands remain on the same bat grip and the barrel stays in the same upper-left lag phase. Their foot silhouettes remain near the fixed contact registration (the prior encoded-foot QA reports all nine selected PRE/contact/POST masks within its limits). As a static authored progression, PRE-high→PRE-middle→PRE-low is coherent for a low-target preparation; PRE-high→PRE-middle is coherent for a middle-target preparation. For the high target, PRE-high alone is the sensible choice. Using extra height poses should begin only after LOCK, when the saved target row is known; it must not expose hidden row information before LOCK.

POST has the complementary order. POST-low→POST-middle→POST-high raises the helmet and trunk while the same two-hand grip carries the bat from the same extended up-right phase; POST-low→POST-middle is a plausible middle-target recovery, and POST-high alone is suitable for high. This is an actual whole-character height progression with consistent identity and grip, not evidence that the full timed animation passes. Keep each cell intact and use authored poses; do not crossfade/morph them. The black chest mark on PRE-high is visible in the montage and absent or much less apparent in the other PREs; compare the source-over render at intended size before accepting it as a detail. It does not change the pose-order finding.

The original frames have distinct jobs, so several should not be treated as generic plant-to-PRE inbetweens. Old5 is a forward-stride/hip-drive drawing and old6 is barrel-lag with head, hands and stance already carried forward. They are valid within the original forward swing, but the shared-cell view shows old6→new PRE moves the head/grip back toward the contact stance while the bat remains in an upper-left lag orientation. Old7 has already brought the bat down-right; placing it before an up-left PRE reverses the bat path. Thus old5/6/7 should not be inserted into a post-LOCK height-preparation chain simply because their names fall between plant and contact. Old4→PRE-high is the least contradictory existing start: both show a loaded/up-left bat; the PRE height row then provides a gradual, target-dependent body compression. The old4→PRE-high join still needs timed, full-size review; the stills alone cannot certify that its shoulder/hip settling is smooth. No further prep drawing is evidenced if that join passes.

On recovery, selected POST poses have arms and bat fully extended up-right. Original9 (`early-follow`) continues in that direction and is the legitimate existing bridge into original10 (`follow`/wrap); original10 should not be jumped to directly from POST. Original9→10 still changes the bat from pointing forward/up-right to being carried across the back/shoulder. Original9 is an authored early-follow pose, but it does not itself depict the intermediate wrap. The shortest credible source order is POST (height-ordered for the selected row)→old9→old10→old11→old12→old13. If the old9→old10 transition remains a visible snap at accepted timing, the exact missing drawing is one common late-follow pose: both hands still gripping, torso/shoulders continuing the turn, and barrel partway from forward/up-right toward the back/shoulder. Do not add a new drawing solely from these stills; first test old9→10 at the real clock. Old11 onward are recovery/return, not substitutes for that bridge.

Do not insert old5/6/7 into these post-LOCK lists without a demonstrated continuous bat/hand/foot path; the source stills show phase/stance conflicts. Do not report a motion pass based on a montage or these order hypotheses.

## Decision

The high→middle→low PRE and low→middle→high POST row orders are plausible authored preparation/recovery progressions and resolve some body-height compression without synthesizing art. They are appropriate candidates for a later frozen-clock local review. HOLD motion/production approval: old4→PRE-high and old9→10 are the two unresolved joins, and neither has been validated in real playback here. If one additional drawing becomes necessary after playback, the specific likely gap is late-follow-to-wrap (old9→old10), not another generic low-contact crouch. No repo, original asset, or game file was changed.
