Read-only review of call6 PRE-high cleanup and call7 common late-follow source at native 1254×1254 and the 768px-per-cell three-pose comparison. ## Call 6: PRE-high chest cleanup — PASS at source level

The isolated high PRE image no longer has the distinct dark/gold chest badge seen in the prior version. At native scale the cream jersey now has normal folds; it does not appear to have a rectangular paint-over, leftover emblem edge, or damaged sleeve/trim. Light and dark composites show no visible correction halo. Helmet, face, hair, uniform, bat, gloves, knees and shoes match the intended PRE-high pose. Both hands remain together at the same grip on one bat; bat length and upper-left lag direction are preserved. Foot QA reports rear/front IoU .9566/.9042 and anchor-edge drift .58/2.31 canonical pixels. These support, but do not alone establish, the visual judgment.

## Call 7: common late-follow pose — PASS as an authored source pose

This reads chronologically between the extended POST/early-follow and original10 wrap. Relative to POST-high/original9, the elbows bend and the joined hands travel upward/inward; the bat lifts to near upright above/beside the helmet while still pointing into the upper-right. Relative to original10, the bat has not yet fallen across the shoulder and the arms are not yet fully folded behind the head. That intermediate bat/arm orientation makes this a plausible late follow-through rather than a return to the load or a second backswing. The torso continues its turn on the same stance. The helmet/head, face/hair, cream/navy/gold uniform, gloves, shin guards and cleats stay consistent. Both white-gloved hands appear connected on one handle; the shaft clears the face/helmet in both light and dark composites. No ball, second bat, motion trail, extra limb, text, guide marks or added emblem is visible.

The three-pose comparison (POST-high→new late-follow→old10) shows a meaningful bat arc, but old10 still makes a sizeable final move across the shoulder. The new pose reduces the missing transition; it does not guarantee that timing/spacing will read at the final animation clock. At canonical 724px scale, the source remains legible and the bat/hand silhouette is not lost. Feet QA reports IoU .7947/.8911 and toe/sole anchor drift 3.46/1.15 canonical pixels; the rear heel lifts/rotates as the stance turns, while its toe and the front sole remain the anchors. Do not describe this as a fixed heel mask.

## Decision

Call6 is a source-level PASS for the requested local detail cleanup. Call7 is a source-level PASS for the requested common late-follow in-between: it has continuous identity, grip and bat and reads as follow-through between the endpoints. Together these two source calls resolve the known PRE-high marking inconsistency and provide a credible old9→late-follow→old10 path. The remaining question is timed motion quality of the sequence, particularly the late-follow→old10 beat; this review does not accept the clock, contact, physical plane, or production integration.
