## Final prompt set (built-in imagegen)

### Batter — transparent_background=true

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset, transparent PNG sprite atlas, NOT a mockup. Create ONE exact 4 columns x 2 rows contact sheet with eight equally sized cells on completely transparent alpha. Each cell contains the SAME adult male right-handed baseball batter from the SAME locked camera behind homeplate, facing away toward pitcher at screen upper right. Camera sees his BACK and right rear quarter, helmet hides virtually all face, NO front-facing portrait. White baseball pants, navy socks/shoes, cream jersey with plain navy number 9 on BACK, navy batting helmet, light wooden bat, athletic adult realistic anatomy. Style: exceptionally crafted hand-pixelled 2D indie game pixel art, clustered deliberate pixels, 4 tone material shading, dark navy outlines, soft warm stadium rimlight from upper right, NO painterly blur, NO neon slash, NO props/floor/chalk/text/grid. Framing EACH cell identical fixed scale, full bat and entire body with padding. Both shoe sole positions stay at same BASELINE 90% down each cell; head top about 24%, raised bat may reach 7%. Right handed baseball stance viewed from BEHIND homeplate: rear right foot is screen LEFT and slightly nearer camera/lower; front left foot is screen RIGHT and slightly farther/higher. Feet stay within compact 55% cell width, no wide cartoon split stance, no mirror flips between poses. Eight cells left-to-right: row1 [READY relaxed bent knees and bat high over rear shoulder, LOAD coil weight onto rear right leg keeping hands by rear shoulder, STRIDE small front left step toward pitcher with rear foot anchored, CONTACT rotated hips and shoulders back facing camera with two gloved hands together driving bat horizontally right toward plate at screen right around waist level]. Row2 [FOLLOW THROUGH bat continuing across left shoulder with rear right heel up, DECELERATE compact balanced finish weight supported by front left leg, RETURN hands lower together and bat back smoothly behind rear shoulder feet returning, READY EXACT same as first frame]. A believable baseball swing sequence with consistent pelvis/head volumes and correct grip, no teleporting hands or feet. Do not draw eight independent characters with different viewpoints. Each cell width and height identical; transparent margins clear; absolutely no labels.

### Stadium — transparent_background=false

Use case: stylized-concept. Production 2D pixel art baseball game environment BACKGROUND asset only. Extremely wide panoramic horizontal 4:1 composition, no UI/text/actors/logos. Premium meticulous hand-pixelled indie art with coherent pixel clusters and 4-tone materials, midnight navy, warm amber floodlights, restrained muted emerald grass and clay dirt. Camera centered behind homeplate looking toward distant center field at eye height2m; the field is NOT a bird's eye diamond and NOT a side-view platform. Correct baseball stadium bowl with symmetrical outfield stands far away, small dusk city silhouette, two floodlight banks at far sides. Compressed telephoto lens. Horizon stadium wall sits about 26% down image; grass playing field from y28% to y72%; unmarked reddish brown clay infield/plate dirt is the entire foreground y72%-100%. Dirt is broad and low contrast. IMPORTANT: DO NOT draw homeplate, batter boxes, any chalk lines, pitching mound, rubber, bases, diamonds or baseball characters anywhere: they are separate runtime layers. The center of grass at x50% y41% must stay visually empty for a separately rendered mound and small pitcher. Perspective grass mowing stripes recede naturally toward center vanishing point at x50% y25%. No repeated sharp grids or noisy tiny glitter. Strong believable ground plane with natural scale and depth, empty foreground for a batter to be composited. The art must remain correctly proportioned, not vertically squashed; use a naturally panoramic stadium camera rather than squeezing a tall image.
