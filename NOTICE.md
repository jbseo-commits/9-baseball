## Artwork optimisation

`assets/runtime-opt/*.webp` are mechanically re-encoded derivatives of the PNG
masters under `assets/`. They are generated by `scripts/optimize-runtime-art.py`
(Pillow) or `scripts/optimize-runtime-art.mjs` (sharp). The PNG masters are the
art; the WebP files carry no independent authorship.

## AI-generated artwork

All game artwork was produced with generative image tooling. Prompts and
provenance are recorded per asset group under `assets/*/README.md`,
`assets/*/PROMPTS.md` and `docs/art/`. See LICENSE for the disclosure terms.
